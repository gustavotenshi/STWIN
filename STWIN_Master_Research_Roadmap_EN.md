# STWIN Master Research Roadmap

Long-term academic program for the Subluminally Transported Wormhole Infrastructure Network

Version 1.0 — Initial state of the program following publication of Paper v1 and the conceptual conclusion on NER/SNT

## 0. Current project status

**Purpose of this document.** To serve as a master map that prevents loss of context, duplicated effort, and disorganized progress. Each domain below may generate independent subprojects, simulations, technical notes, and papers.

- ✅ STWIN v1 conceptual paper published — Completed

- ✅ Initial temporal problem: proper-time desynchronization — Conceptually concluded

- ✅ Network Epoch Reset (NER) — Conceptually adopted

- ✅ STWIN Network Time (SNT) — Adopted as an operational layer

- ✅ Forward-Time Operational Principle — Conceptually adopted

- ⏳ Physical realization of NER/SNT — Open

- ⏳ Two-mouth physical causality — Next step

- ⏳ Moving-mouth dynamics — Future

- ⏳ Stress-energy / exotic energy — Future

- ⏳ Information, networks, safety and security, transport, and expansion — Future

Methodological rules:

- Work on one central question at a time.

- Distinguish established physics, known theoretical results, conditional extrapolations, STWIN hypotheses, and engineering speculation.

- Try to falsify each hypothesis before attempting to defend it.

- Record the conclusion, confidence level, objections, and open questions at the end of each stage.

- Advance only when it is clear what has been concluded and what remains open.
